How the buckets were assigned · sources & limits

“Revise the approach” counts the first lease after each substantive revision. Later unchanged leases are “Continue unchanged.” Initial smoke and branch-comparison leases remain “Initial experiments,” including their continuations. Diagnostic forks form a separate bucket. These are analyst-assigned purposes, not native phase labels.

There is no measured duration for coding, exploration or model thinking in this figure. A zero means no allocated experiment was assigned to that bucket; it does not mean the agent never considered that behavior.

All setup, serialization, idle worker time, failures and teardown stay inside each allocation. The rejected Sol practice request launched no worker and adds no compute. Three rejected Sol source reads also add no compute. Kimi’s incomplete public practice remains included.

Public practice used small, reusable panels. Changes in architecture, training duration, random streams and hyperparameters were not isolated causal ablations. No private final score was available during research.
