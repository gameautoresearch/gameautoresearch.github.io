# Damage breakpoints in the fixed level-11 mode

Minimum number of clean full-damage connections needed to defeat a full-health target, with no intervening hits. Ceil(HP/damage). These are arithmetic checks, not a strategy recommendation; deployment, movement, target legality and attack timings still matter. Arrows is shown as a full three-wave cast; losing a wave changes the result. Spell crown reductions are separate.

Giant cannot attack the troop targets shown; its row is an arithmetic illustration only, **not a legal interaction**. Normal tower and troop damage has no crown reduction; Fireball crown damage is159, full Arrows crown damage75 (25 per wave), Zap crown damage48. These values use the compiled pinned data.
