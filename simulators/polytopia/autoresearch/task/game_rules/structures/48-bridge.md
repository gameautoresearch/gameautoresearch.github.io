# Bridge

Bridge requires a shallow-water tile with opposite land endpoints. North-south span wins if both spans exist. Eligible neutral construction is allowed. Without Fishing, entering straight across the span from the side is blocked (diagonals may be allowed). A carried unit on a bridge can disembark in place.
