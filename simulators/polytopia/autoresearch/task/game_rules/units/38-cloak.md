# Cloak

Purchased with Diplomacy. Moves with Creep/Hide/Scout and infiltrates cities rather than doing a zero-damage ordinary attack. Infiltration consumes the Cloak, steals raw city production, creates riot and up to five Daggers/Pirates. Static and Stiff restrict promotion/retaliation.
