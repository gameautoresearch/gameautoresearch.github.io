# MindBender

Uses conversion instead of normal damage attacks. Conversion transfers ownership and full unit score, exhausts the converted unit and rehomes it to the current capital unless Independent. HealOthers heals adjacent own units by four or clears poison without healing that action. Stiff prevents retaliation.

Base table HP is overridden by a passenger’s max HP for ordinary transport boats; veteran passengers add5 HP. Nominal costs are also used for score and disband refunds, not a promise that this form is directly purchasable.
