## Limits

- Representative rule examples, not all possible hands or sequences.
- No compiled-native or resident-CUDA trajectories executed by documentation work.
- Generator/detector/selector ambiguities preserved and explained rather than normalized to another platform variant.

The written rules apply to the pinned mode identified above. Catalog membership does not by itself establish that every combination of effects has been independently tested.
