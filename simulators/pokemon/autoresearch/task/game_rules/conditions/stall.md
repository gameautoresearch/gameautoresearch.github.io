# stall condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

Tracks consecutive protection attempts. Repeated protect-family actions have success 1/3, 1/9, etc. after the first eligible success; the shared counter and chance floor apply across repeated protection attempts.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |

| Duration | 2 |
| Counter maximum | 729 |

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
