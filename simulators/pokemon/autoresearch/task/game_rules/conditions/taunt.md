# Taunt condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

Prevents the target from using non-damaging moves for its next three turns. Pokemon with the Oblivious Ability or protected by the Aroma Veil Ability are immune.

This is the persistent condition created by [Taunt](../moves/taunt.md), rather than a separately selectable move.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 3 |
| Residual resolution order | 15 |
| Before move resolution priority | 5 |

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
