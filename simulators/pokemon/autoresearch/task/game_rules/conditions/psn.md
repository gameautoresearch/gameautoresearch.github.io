[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

Regular poison: loses 1/8 base maximum HP each residual turn. Poison/Steel types normally resist poison status unless an exception such as Corrosion applies.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Residual resolution order | 9 |

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
