# Revival Blessing condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

A fainted party member is selected and revived with 1/2 its max HP, rounded down. Fails if there are no fainted party members.

This is the persistent condition created by [Revival Blessing](../moves/revivalblessing.md), rather than a separately selectable move.
