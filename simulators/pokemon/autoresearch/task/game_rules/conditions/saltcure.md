# Salt Cure condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

Causes damage to the target equal to 1/8 of its maximum HP (1/4 if the target is Steel or Water type), rounded down, at the end of each turn during effect. This effect ends when the target is no longer active.

This is the persistent condition created by [Salt Cure](../moves/saltcure.md), rather than a separately selectable move.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Residual resolution order | 13 |
