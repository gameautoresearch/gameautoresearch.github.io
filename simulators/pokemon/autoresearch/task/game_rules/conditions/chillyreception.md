# Chilly Reception condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

For 5 turns, the weather becomes Snow. The user switches out even if it is trapped and is replaced immediately by a selected party member. The user does not switch out if there are no unfainted party members.

This is the persistent condition created by [Chilly Reception](../moves/chillyreception.md), rather than a separately selectable move.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 1 |
| Before move resolution priority | 100 |

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
