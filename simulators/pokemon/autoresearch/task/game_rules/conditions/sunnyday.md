# sunnyday condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

Sun lasts five turns, or eight with the appropriate extending rock. It multiplies Fire damage by 1.5 and Water damage by 0.5 and activates sun-dependent abilities/moves.
