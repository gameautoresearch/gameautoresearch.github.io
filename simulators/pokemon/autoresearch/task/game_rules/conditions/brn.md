# brn condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

Burn: loses 1/16 of base maximum HP each residual turn. Physical move damage is halved unless the relevant exception applies (Guts, Facade). Fire types normally cannot be burned.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Residual resolution order | 10 |
