# mustrecharge condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

The user must spend the next applicable action recharging instead of selecting an ordinary attack after the source move.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 2 |
| Before move resolution priority | 11 |
| Lock move | recharge |

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
