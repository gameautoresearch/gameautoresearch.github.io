# confusion condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

Confusion is volatile. Before the move, the timer decrements; if still confused there is a 33/100 chance to hit oneself for typeless 40-power physical confusion damage and lose that action. The normal initial counter is 2–5 (Axe Kick 3–5), giving the corresponding remaining opportunities before the cure check. Switching normally clears confusion.
