# flinch condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

Flinch prevents the affected Pokémon’s action if it has not already acted this turn. It lasts one turn; relevant abilities can react to or prevent flinching.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 1 |
| Before move resolution priority | 8 |

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
