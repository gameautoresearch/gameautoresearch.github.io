# tox condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

Bad poison: residual damage uses floor(baseMaxHP/16), at least one, multiplied by an increasing stage from 1 through 15. The escalating stage resets on switching in; the poisoned status itself persists.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Residual resolution order | 9 |

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
