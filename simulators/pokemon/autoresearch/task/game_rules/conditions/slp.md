## Effect and lifetime

Sleep: normally prevents ordinary moves for 1–3 action opportunities. The engine samples an internal counter 2,3,4, then decrements before action; waking occurs when it reaches zero. Early Bird decrements an extra time. Sleep Talk is sleep-usable. Rest has its own fixed sleep behavior. Sleep Clause Mod prevents a second living foe put to sleep by the opposing side.
