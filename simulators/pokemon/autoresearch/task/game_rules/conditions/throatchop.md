# Throat Chop condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

For 2 turns, the target cannot use sound-based moves.

This is the persistent condition created by [Throat Chop](../moves/throatchop.md), rather than a separately selectable move.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 2 |
| Before move resolution priority | 6 |
| Residual resolution order | 22 |

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
