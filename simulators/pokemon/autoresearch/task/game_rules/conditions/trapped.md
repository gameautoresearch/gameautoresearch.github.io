# trapped condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

The effect calls the engine trap check and prevents ordinary switching when applicable. Ghost typing, Shed Shell and effect-specific escape conditions can override trapping. A hidden trapping ability can be discovered only when a choice is refused.

## Duration and timing details

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
