# Glaive Rush condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

If this move is successful, moves targeted at the user deal double damage and do not check accuracy until the user's next turn.

This is the persistent condition created by [Glaive Rush](../moves/glaiverush.md), rather than a separately selectable move.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Before move resolution priority | 100 |

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
