# choicelock condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

When an active Choice item applies, move selection is restricted to the recorded move. Switching, item changes/suppression and disabled/zero-PP cases can release or alter the restriction.

## Duration and timing details

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
