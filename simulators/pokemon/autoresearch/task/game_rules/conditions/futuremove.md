# futuremove condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

Stores a delayed Future Sight/Doom Desire-style attack on a side slot and resolves it at the scheduled residual stage; the current occupant receives its target check.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Residual resolution order | 3 |

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
