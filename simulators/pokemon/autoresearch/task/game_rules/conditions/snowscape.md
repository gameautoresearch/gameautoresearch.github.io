# snowscape condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

Snow lasts five turns, or eight with Icy Rock. In Gen 9 it boosts Ice-type Defense by 1.5 and does not deal old hail chip damage. Snow interacts with Blizzard, Aurora Veil and snow abilities.
