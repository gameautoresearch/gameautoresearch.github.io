## Effect and lifetime

Binding moves hold the target and deal residual damage while the source/effect remains valid. Normal duration is randomly four or five turns; Grip Claw and Binding Band modify duration/damage where present. See the trapping move for its particular rules.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 5 |
| Residual resolution order | 13 |

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
