# raindance condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

Rain lasts five turns, or eight when set by the appropriate extending rock. It multiplies Water damage by 1.5 and Fire damage by 0.5 and activates rain-dependent abilities/moves.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |

| Duration | 5 |
