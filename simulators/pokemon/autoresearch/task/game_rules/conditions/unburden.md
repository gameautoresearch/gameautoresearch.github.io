# Unburden condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

If this Pokemon loses its held item for any reason, its Speed is doubled as long as it remains active, has this Ability, and is not holding an item.

This is the persistent condition created by [Unburden](../abilities/unburden.md), rather than a separately selectable move.

## Duration and timing details

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
