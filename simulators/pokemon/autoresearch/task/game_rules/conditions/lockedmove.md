# lockedmove condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

Locks the user into a repeated move such as Outrage for its rolled duration. Completion can induce confusion; interruptions, failed turns and ability interactions use the named move and move-specific exceptions.

## Duration and timing details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 2 |

Durations are measured in battle turns. Switching, fainting or the stated effect-specific conditions may end an effect before its maximum duration.
