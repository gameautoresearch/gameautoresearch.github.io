# sandstorm condition

[Condition index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect and lifetime

Sandstorm lasts five turns, or eight with Smooth Rock. It deals 1/16 base-max-HP residual damage to susceptible active Pokémon and boosts Rock-type Special Defense by 1.5; immunities are effect-specific.
