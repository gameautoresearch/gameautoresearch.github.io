# Eviolite

[Item index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

If holder's species can evolve, its Defense and Sp. Def are 1.5×.

**Scope:** Conservative candidate emitted by a pinned singles item selector or required form item. Branch candidates are not asserted equally likely or reachable for every species.
