# Serene Grace

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

This Pokemon's moves have their secondary effect chance doubled. This effect stacks with the Rainbow effect, except for secondary effects that cause the target to flinch.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.

| Modify move resolution priority | -2 |
