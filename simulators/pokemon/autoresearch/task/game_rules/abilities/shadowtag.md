# Shadow Tag

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

Prevents opposing Pokemon from choosing to switch out, unless they are holding a Shed Shell, are a Ghost type, or also have this Ability.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
