# Poison Touch

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

This Pokemon's contact moves have a 30% chance of poisoning. This effect comes after a move's inherent secondary effect chance.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
