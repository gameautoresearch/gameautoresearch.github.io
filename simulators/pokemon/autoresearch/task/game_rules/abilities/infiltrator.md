# Infiltrator

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

This Pokemon's moves ignore substitutes and the opposing side's Reflect, Light Screen, Safeguard, Mist, and Aurora Veil.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
