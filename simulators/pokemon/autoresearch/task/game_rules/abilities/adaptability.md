# Adaptability

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

This Pokemon's moves that match one of its types have a same-type attack bonus (STAB) of 2 instead of 1.5.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
