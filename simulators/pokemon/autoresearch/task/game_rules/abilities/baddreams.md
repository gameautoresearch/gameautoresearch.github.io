# Bad Dreams

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

Causes opposing Pokemon to lose 1/8 of their maximum HP, rounded down, at the end of each turn if they are asleep.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
