# Reckless

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

This Pokemon's attacks with recoil or crash damage have their power multiplied by 1.2. Does not affect Struggle.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
