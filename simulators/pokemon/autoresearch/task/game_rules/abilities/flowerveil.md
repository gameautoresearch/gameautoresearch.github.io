# Flower Veil

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

Grass-type Pokemon on this Pokemon's side cannot have their stat stages lowered by other Pokemon or have a non-volatile status condition inflicted on them by other Pokemon.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.

## Additional rule details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Can be bypassed by ability-ignoring effects | Yes |
