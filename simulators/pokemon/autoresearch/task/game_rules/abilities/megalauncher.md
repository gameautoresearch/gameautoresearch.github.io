# Mega Launcher

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

This Pokemon's pulse moves have their power multiplied by 1.5. Heal Pulse restores 3/4 of a target's maximum HP, rounded half down.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
