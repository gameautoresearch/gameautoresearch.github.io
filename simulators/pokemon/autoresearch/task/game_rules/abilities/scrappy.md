# Scrappy

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

This Pokemon can hit Ghost types with Normal- and Fighting-type moves. This Pokemon is immune to the effect of the Intimidate Ability.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
