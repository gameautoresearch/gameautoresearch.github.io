# Magnet Pull

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

Prevents opposing Steel-type Pokemon from choosing to switch out, unless they are holding a Shed Shell or are a Ghost type.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
