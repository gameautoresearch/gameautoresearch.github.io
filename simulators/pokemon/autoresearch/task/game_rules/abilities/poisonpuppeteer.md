# Poison Puppeteer

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

If this Pokemon is a Pecharunt and poisons or badly poisons a target, the target also becomes confused.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
