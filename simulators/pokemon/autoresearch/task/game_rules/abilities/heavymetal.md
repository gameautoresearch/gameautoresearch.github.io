# Heavy Metal

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

This Pokemon's weight is doubled. This effect is calculated after the effect of Autotomize, and before the effect of Float Stone.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.

## Additional rule details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Can be bypassed by ability-ignoring effects | Yes |
| Modify weight resolution priority | 1 |
