# Comatose

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

This Pokemon is considered to be asleep and cannot become affected by a non-volatile status condition or Yawn.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
