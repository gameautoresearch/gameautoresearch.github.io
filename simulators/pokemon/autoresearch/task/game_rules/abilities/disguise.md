# Disguise

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

If this Pokemon is a Mimikyu, the first hit it takes in battle deals 0 neutral damage. Its disguise is then broken, it changes to Busted Form, and it loses 1/8 of its max HP. Confusion damage also breaks the disguise.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.

## Additional rule details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Can be bypassed by ability-ignoring effects | Yes |
| Damage resolution priority | 1 |
