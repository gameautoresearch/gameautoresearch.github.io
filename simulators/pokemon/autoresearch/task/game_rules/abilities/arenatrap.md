# Arena Trap

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

Prevents opposing Pokemon from choosing to switch out unless they are airborne, are holding a Shed Shell, or are a Ghost type.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
