# Weak Armor

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

If a physical attack hits this Pokemon, its Defense is lowered by 1 stage and its Speed is raised by 2 stages.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
