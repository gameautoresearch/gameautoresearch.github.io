# Defiant

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

This Pokemon's Attack is raised by 2 stages for each of its stat stages that is lowered by an opposing Pokemon.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
