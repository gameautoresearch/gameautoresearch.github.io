# Cute Charm

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

There is a 30% chance a Pokemon making contact with this Pokemon will become infatuated if it is of the opposite gender.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
