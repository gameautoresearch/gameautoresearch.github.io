# Sticky Hold

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

This Pokemon cannot lose its held item due to another Pokemon's Ability or attack, unless the attack knocks out this Pokemon. A Sticky Barb will be transferred to other Pokemon regardless of this Ability.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.

## Additional rule details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Can be bypassed by ability-ignoring effects | Yes |
