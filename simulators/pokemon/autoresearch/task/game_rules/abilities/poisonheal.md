# Poison Heal

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

If this Pokemon is poisoned, it restores 1/8 of its maximum HP, rounded down, at the end of each turn instead of losing HP.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.
