# Air Lock

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

While this Pokemon is active, the effects of weather conditions are disabled.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.

| Rule detail | Value |

| Suppresses weather effects | Yes |
