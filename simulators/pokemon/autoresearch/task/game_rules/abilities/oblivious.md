# Oblivious

[Ability index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

## Effect

This Pokemon cannot be infatuated or taunted. Gaining this Ability while infatuated or taunted cures it. This Pokemon is immune to the effect of the Intimidate Ability.

**Scope:** Selectable in at least one initial singles Random Battle role template.

## Additional rule details

| Rule detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Can be bypassed by ability-ignoring effects | Yes |
