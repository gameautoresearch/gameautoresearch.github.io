# Revival Blessing

[Move index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

**Mode status:** Present in at least one pinned singles set movepool.

## Effect

A fainted party member is selected and revived with 1/2 its max HP, rounded down. Fails if there are no fainted party members.
