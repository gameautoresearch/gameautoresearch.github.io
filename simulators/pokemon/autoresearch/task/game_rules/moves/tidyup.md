# Tidy Up

[Move index](README.md) · [Wiki home](../README.md)

**Mode status:** Present in at least one pinned singles set movepool.

## Effect

Raises the user's Attack and Speed by 1 stage. Removes substitutes from all active Pokemon and ends the effects of Spikes, Stealth Rock, Sticky Web, and Toxic Spikes for both sides.
