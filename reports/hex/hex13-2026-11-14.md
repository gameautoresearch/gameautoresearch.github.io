# Hex moves to 13x13, with a per-move inference cap — 2026-11-14

Local, offline work. No Modal resource, image build, GPU allocation or model call was made. Nothing is
committed. No 13x13 session has run yet.

## Why

In the six-researcher suite of 2026-11-13 the two best Hex researchers beat the 9x9 anchor (MoHex at
200,000 games with its solver, close to perfect play on that board) 55.5% and 57% of the time
(`reports/suite-2026-11-13.md`). A harder opponent could not have helped: a perfect player scores at most
50% against a perfect anchor when every opening is played from both seats. The owner's decisions:

1. Hex is 13x13 from now on.
2. Inference is capped per move.
3. Every campaign ends with a head-to-head tournament, run as soon as the networks are done.

- Gaps are 180 to 420 Elo, less even than the 9x9 ladder's 250.

- The anchor is not perfect play. The score's ceiling is 100, and the tournament is what ranks entrants
  that pass the anchor.

## The move clock

A call covering n positions must answer within 0.1 + 0.01 n seconds on one CPU thread: 0.74 s for a
full batch of 64, 0.11 s for one position. A late call still plays random moves for its games.

One-thread forward passes on 13x13, 64 positions, on the laptop while it was busy with other work:

| Residual network | Parameters | 64 positions | Fits 0.74 s |
|---|---|---|---|
| 48 channels, 4 blocks | 0.17 M | 0.12 s | about 6 passes |
| 64 channels, 6 blocks | 0.45 M | 0.34 s | about 2 passes |
| 96 channels, 8 blocks | 1.3 M | 0.9 s | not quite one |
| 128 channels, 10 blocks | 3.0 M | 0.85 s | not quite one |

So the cap allows a few passes of networks the size last suite's winners used, or one pass of something
larger, and no deep search. Sol's 9x9 winner budgeted 2.6 s of search per call.

## Not measured, or still open

- **The clock's two numbers are my choice** from the table above, on a loaded laptop. The Modal
  evaluation CPU has not been timed, and the last two rows of the table are noisy. One line changes them.
- **A one-position call gets 0.11 s**, about ten times the per-position allowance of a full batch. Calls
  that small only happen as a schedule's last games finish.
- **Practice and grading time on Modal are unmeasured.** An anchor game lasted about 75 plies in
  calibration. The 1,800 s practice grant is a generous guess; at $1.52 an hour a full grant is $0.76 of
  the $10 budget.
- **Whether 13x13 resists a $10 session is a prediction**, not a result.
- **The nanny's tournament step was checked in a local simulation** with stand-in commands, not on Modal.
  Network-session resolution was checked against last night's real Hex, Generals and Polytopia sessions.
- The Hex tournament on 13x13 with five references plays reference-vs-reference games as well; its
  running time is unmeasured.
- Generals was not touched here (another session is building its learned panel).
